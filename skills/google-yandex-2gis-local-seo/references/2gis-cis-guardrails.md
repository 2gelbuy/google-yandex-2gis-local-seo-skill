# 2GIS/CIS Local Presence Guardrails

Checked: 2026-05-20.

## Positioning

2GIS is a CIS/Kazakhstan local discovery platform and business directory, not a
minor citation afterthought. For CIS local SEO work, treat 2GIS as a peer local
presence surface beside Google Business Profile and Yandex Business.

Use 2GIS only from verified business facts. Do not create or optimize cards for
fake branches, hidden locations, invented services, fake reviews, unsupported
prices, copied photos, or keyword-stuffed rubrics.

## Official 2GIS Sources

- Adding a company to 2GIS is free. A company representative or any user can
  submit a missing company, but 2GIS checks the information before publishing.
  For representatives, 2GIS says specialists call back within three business
  days to verify data; online/mobile updates appear after checking, while PC
  directory updates can lag until the next monthly version.
  Source: https://help.2gis.ru/question/kak-dobavit-kompaniyu-v-2gis
- Company representatives should use the 2GIS business account to keep company
  data current, add extra information, choose rubrics, upload photos, and work
  with customer reviews. Access to the business account is described as free.
  An alternate update form exists when the cabinet is inconvenient, with 2GIS
  verification before directory changes are applied.
  Source: https://help.2gis.ru/question/kak-vnesti-izmeneniya-vnbspkartochku-kompanii
- 2GIS correction flows can update organization facts including work schedule,
  separate street entrance, office/floor/intercom, phone, messengers, email,
  website, social accounts, rubrics, name/address changes, service details, and
  accessibility information. 2GIS recommends attaching links, photos, or
  screenshots to speed processing.
  Source: https://help.2gis.ru/question/datafeedback-more-info
- A standard free 2GIS company card contains reference information such as
  address, hours, phone, site and social links, photos, payment methods,
  services, reviews, and other facts. All companies can be added free.
  Source: https://reklama.2gis.ru/card
- Official 2GIS geoservice guidance prioritizes accurate activity/service
  coverage, current contact methods and hours, entrance data, informative
  photos, visible goods/services with prices, and real customer reviews. It
  explicitly says listed spheres and services must match reality.
  Source: https://reklama.2gis.ru/promotion-in-geoservice
- Photo uploads require a registered user and pass moderation, usually in one to
  two business days. Users can delete only their own photos; rule-violating
  third-party photos should be reported to moderators.
  Source: https://help.2gis.ru/question/kak-dobavit-foto-k-kartochke-kompanii-ilizdaniya
- Reviews are written by authorized 2GIS or Flamp users. Users leave reviews
  inside the company card, can attach photos, and cannot write multiple reviews
  for the same place or branch; they should edit the existing review instead.
  Complaints about review/photo violations go to moderators.
  Source: https://help.2gis.ru/question/kak-ostavit-svoy-otzyv-k-kompanii

## Audit Checklist

For each real branch/location:

1. Card exists for the real branch and city.
2. Owner/business-account access state is known.
3. Name matches the real-world brand without keyword stuffing.
4. Address, map point, entrance, office/floor/intercom, and city are correct.
5. Phone, messengers, email, website, and social links match site/GBP/Yandex.
6. Hours and holiday/special hours are current.
7. Rubrics/services reflect what the branch actually offers.
8. Products/services/prices are present only when owner-verified and visible.
9. Photos are real, current, useful, and acceptable for moderation.
10. Reviews and owner responses are monitored without fake reviews or gating.
11. Duplicate, stale, closed, or wrong-city cards are flagged for cleanup.

## Safe Actions

- Read-only profile/card audit.
- NAP comparison against site, GBP, Yandex Business, Yandex Maps, and schema.
- Draft owner-submitted corrections with evidence links/photos/screenshots.
- Draft photo checklist and review-response templates.
- Draft products/services list only from verified site or owner data.

## Approval-Gated Actions

- Claiming access, submitting a new company, updating card data, changing
  rubrics, uploading photos, adding products/services/prices, replying to
  reviews, filing duplicate/closure reports, or using paid 2GIS ads.
